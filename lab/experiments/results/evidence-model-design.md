## 1. Executive Summary
ORION-044 formalizes the 11-category Epistemic Evidence Taxonomy for APE, strictly separating STATED_INTENT (survey optimism) from OBSERVED_BEHAVIOR (verified execution/payment). Single numeric confidence percentages are replaced by multi-dimensional epistemic coverage metrics.

## 3. Current Evidence Model Critique
Uniform hypothesis scoring (treating H1 Problem, H2 Payment, and H3 Trial as equal survey counts) is flawed. Payment and trial intents require behavioral proof (OBSERVED_PAYMENT, OBSERVED_USAGE), whereas survey responses are merely STATED_INTENT (weight 0.3x).

## 4. Proposed 11-Category Evidence Taxonomy

| Category | Definition | Epistemic Weight | Can Trigger GO? | Can Trigger NO-GO? |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| `OBSERVED_BEHAVIOR` | User reproduced setup problem or executed CLI binary. | `1.0` | `True` | `False` |
| `OBSERVED_PAYMENT` | User has active SaaS/tool spend ($/month) for current problem. | `1.0` | `True` | `False` |
| `OBSERVED_USAGE` | User maintains an active manual workaround daily/weekly. | `0.8` | `False` | `False` |
| `STATED_INTENT` | User survey response stating hypothetical payment ('Would pay $29') or trial interest. | `0.3` | `False` | `False` |
| `REPORTED_BEHAVIOR` | User describes past manual labor hours during direct interview. | `0.6` | `False` | `False` |
| `NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE` | User explicitly denies problem ('don't have problem', 'won't pay', 'current tools fine'). | `1.0` | `False` | `True` |
| `UNKNOWN` | Missing survey fields or zero responses logged. | `0.0` | `False` | `False` |
| `INFERRED` | Analytical hypothesis or conclusion generated by prior internal APE reports. | `0.0` | `False` | `False` |
| `PROVISIONAL_THRESHOLD` | Internal candidate heuristic count (e.g. 10 users). Not a scientific market law. | `0.0` | `False` | `False` |
| `SYNTHETIC` | AI-generated bot response (is_synthetic=true). Ingestion rejected. | `0.0` | `False` | `True` |
| `TEST_FIXTURE` | Controlled test payload used in-memory for simulation testing. | `0.0` | `False` | `False` |

## 5. Evidence Diversity & Sampling Analysis
To mitigate Channel & Selection Bias, APE requires corroboration across $\ge 2$ independent sources (e.g. HN + Reddit + Direct Interview) before evaluating high-confidence evidence.

## 6. Decision Contract & Epistemic Conditions
- `0 Responses` $\rightarrow$ `VALIDATE_MORE` (EMPTY != NEGATIVE).
- `Dominant Negative Evidence` $\rightarrow$ `NO-GO`.
- `STATED_INTENT Alone` $\rightarrow$ CANNOT trigger `GO` without `OBSERVED_BEHAVIOR`.
- `OBSERVED_BEHAVIOR + Corroboration` $\rightarrow$ Prerequisite for `GO` candidate.

## 7. Confidence Model Critique
Single numeric confidence scores (e.g. 35% at 0 responses) create false precision. APE MUST use multi-dimensional coverage metrics (completeness, quality, diversity, coverage, uncertainty) to clearly convey epistemic risk to human engineers.
